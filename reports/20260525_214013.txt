**Objections:**

1. **Tesla's Unique Selling Proposition (USP)**: Tesla's focus on sustainable energy and electric vehicles, combined with its innovative technology and software updates, sets it apart from traditional automakers like Mercedes.

3. **Over-the-Air Software Updates**: Tesla's ability to update its vehicles' software over-the-air ensures that customers have access to the latest features and technology, without the need for physical updates.

By highlighting these quick wins, addressing potential objections, and emphasizing Tesla's differentiators, we can effectively compete against Mercedes in the luxury EV market.
